# Store Attendance and Timekeeping

**Policy Number:** POL-007
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

Fictional policy for the Meridian retail hackathon demo; not real company guidance.

## 1. Clocking In and Out

Clock in at your assigned store terminal at the beginning of your scheduled shift and clock out at the end. Record meal breaks using the same system. Record only your own working time; do not clock in for another employee.

## 2. Missed Clock-In or Clock-Out

If you forgot to clock in or out, tell your shift supervisor and submit a Time Correction request in the employee HRIS. Enter the shift date, actual start and finish times, break times, and a short explanation. Your supervisor verifies the hours before Payroll updates the record. Do not change another employee's time record.

## 3. Running Late

If you expect to arrive late, contact your shift supervisor as soon as possible, preferably before the shift begins. Give your expected arrival time. The supervisor arranges coverage and records the update. If you cannot reach the supervisor, contact the store manager.

## 4. Reporting an Absence

Notify your direct manager or shift supervisor before your shift begins. For sudden illness or emergencies, contact them as soon as reasonably possible. Follow the Sick and Emergency Leave policy for the leave request; notifying a colleague does not replace notifying your supervisor.

## 5. Attendance Record Corrections

Review your attendance record in the employee HRIS. Request a correction from your supervisor if the dates or hours are wrong, and contact the HR Support Desk if the discrepancy remains unresolved. A disputed entry must be reviewed by a person; this assistant cannot change attendance records.
