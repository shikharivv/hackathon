# Sick and Emergency Leave Requests

**Policy Number:** POL-011
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

Fictional policy for the Meridian retail hackathon demo; not real company guidance.

## 1. Applying for Sick Leave

If you are ill and unable to work, notify your direct manager or shift supervisor before your shift, or as soon as reasonably possible if the illness is sudden. Submit a Sick Leave request in the employee HRIS with the dates of absence. Follow HR's instructions about supporting evidence; do not share medical records with this chatbot. Your personal entitlement depends on your employment terms and applicable company policy; this demo does not set a number of paid sick days.

## 2. Emergency Leave

If an urgent personal or family situation prevents attendance, contact your manager or shift supervisor as soon as possible. Explain the expected dates of absence and submit an Emergency Leave request in the employee HRIS when you can. Your manager and HR review the request and the appropriate leave category. The chatbot cannot approve the leave or promise that it is paid.

## 3. Supporting Documents

HR tells you if documentation is needed for your request and how to submit it securely. Upload it only through the approved HRIS or HR channel. Do not put a medical diagnosis, identity document, or another person's private information into the chatbot.

## 4. Returning After Absence

Notify your manager of your expected return date. If you need a change to the return date or work arrangements, discuss it with your manager and HR. A personal accommodation or fitness-for-work decision must be reviewed by an authorized person; the assistant does not make that decision.
