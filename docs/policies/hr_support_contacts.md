# HR Support and Escalation

**Policy Number:** POL-012
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

Fictional policy for the Meridian retail hackathon demo; not real company guidance.

## 1. General HR Support

The HR Support Desk handles policy questions, leave requests, employee profile changes, and questions the assistant cannot resolve. Use the employee HRIS Support area and choose HR Help. For this fictional demo, the contact address is hr-support@meridian.example. This is a sample address, not a monitored service.

## 2. Payroll Support

For payslips, missing hours, deductions, or payment corrections, choose Payroll Query in the employee HRIS. Include the pay period and request reference; do not send banking information to the chatbot. The demo contact is payroll@meridian.example, which is a sample address, not a monitored service.

## 3. Store and Shift Support

Your shift supervisor or store manager is the first contact for rosters, shift swaps, lateness, breaks, and store coverage. Use the store's approved staff contact channel. If your issue remains unresolved, the HR Support Desk can help you find the appropriate reviewer.

## 4. Confidential Workplace Concerns

You may report a workplace concern directly to HR or the company Ethics channel, including when the concern involves your manager. Use the reporting channels in the Code of Conduct. The demo ethics address is ethics@meridian.example and is not monitored. If there is immediate danger, use the relevant local emergency or store safety procedure; this chatbot is not an emergency service.

## 5. When the Assistant Is Unsure

If the documented policies do not answer a question, the assistant asks for relevant clarification or directs the employee to the correct human team. It must not invent entitlements, forms, approval outcomes, or contacts. For a personal case, use the approved HR support channel so an authorized person can review the circumstances.
