# Payroll and Expense Requests

**Policy Number:** POL-008
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

Fictional policy for the Meridian retail hackathon demo; not real company guidance.

## 1. Finding Your Payslip

Open the employee HRIS, choose Payroll, then Payslips, and select the relevant pay period. If you cannot sign in or the payslip is missing, contact Payroll Support through the HR Support Desk. This assistant cannot access your salary or individual payslips.

## 2. Incorrect Pay or Missing Hours

Compare the pay period, recorded shifts, approved overtime, and deductions with your attendance record. Submit a Payroll Query in the employee HRIS and identify the pay period and discrepancy. Your supervisor confirms any missing work hours; Payroll investigates the correction. Do not post bank details or full payslips in this chatbot.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement

Get your manager's approval before incurring a business expense. In the employee HRIS, open Expenses and create an Expense Claim. Attach an itemized receipt, purchase date, amount, business purpose, and approving manager. Submit within 30 calendar days of the expense. Personal purchases are not reimbursable under this demo policy.

## 4. Bank Details

Use the secure employee HRIS to update payment information. Contact Payroll Support if you cannot complete the change. Do not send bank account numbers to this chatbot. Payroll confirms when the change takes effect; the assistant cannot promise it will apply to a particular pay cycle.

## 5. Tracking a Claim

Check the status of a Payroll Query or Expense Claim in the employee HRIS. If a request needs additional evidence, supply it through that secure system. Contact Payroll Support with the request reference if the status is unclear.
