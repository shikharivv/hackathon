## 1. Purpose

This handbook describes the comprehensive benefits package offered to eligible employees of Meridian Technologies, Inc. ("the Company"). The Company is committed to supporting employees' physical, financial, and personal well-being through competitive benefits programs.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 General Eligibility

2.1.1. Regular full-time employees (scheduled for 30+ hours per week) are eligible for all benefits described in this handbook.

2.1.2. Regular part-time employees (scheduled for 20–29 hours per week) are eligible for health insurance, dental, vision, EAP, and pro-rated wellness stipend.

2.1.3. Temporary employees and contractors are not eligible for benefits under this handbook.

### 2.2 Effective Date of Coverage

2.2.1. Benefits coverage begins on the **first day of the month following the employee's start date**.

2.2.2. Employees who start on the first business day of the month will have coverage effective on that date.

### 2.3 Dependent Eligibility

Eligible dependents include:
- Legal spouse or domestic partner
- Biological, adopted, or step-children under age 26
- Children over age 26 who are incapacitated and financially dependent

## 3. Health Insurance

### 3.1 Plan Options

The Company offers three health insurance tiers through its group plan:

| Plan | Monthly Employee Premium | Monthly Family Premium | Deductible (Individual/Family) | Out-of-Pocket Max |
|------|--------------------------|------------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------|
| Basic | $50 | $200 | $2,000 / $4,000 | $6,000 / $12,000 |
| Plus | $125 | $375 | $1,000 / $2,000 | $4,000 / $8,000 |
| Premium | $200 | $550 | $500 / $1,000 | $2,500 / $5,000 |

### 3.2 Coverage Details

3.2.1. All plans include preventive care at 100% coverage (no copay).

3.2.2. In-network office visits: Basic ($40 copay), Plus ($25 copay), Premium ($15 copay).

3.2.3. Prescription drug coverage is included in all tiers with a formulary-based copay structure:
- Generic: $10 / $10 / $5
- Preferred Brand: $35 / $25 / $20
- Non-Preferred Brand: $60 / $50 / $40
- Specialty: 20% coinsurance up to $250 per fill

3.2.4. Mental health services are covered at parity with medical services across all tiers.

### 3.3 Company Contribution

The Company pays **75%** of the employee-only premium and **50%** of the dependent premium across all tiers.

## 4. Dental Insurance

### 4.1 Coverage

4.1.1. The Company provides a single dental plan with the following annual benefits:

- Preventive (cleanings, exams, X-rays): 100% covered, 2 visits per year
- Basic Restorative (fillings, extractions): 80% covered after $50 deductible
- Major Restorative (crowns, bridges, root canals): 50% covered after $50 deductible
- Orthodontia (dependents under 19): 50% covered, $2,000 lifetime maximum

4.1.2. Annual maximum benefit: **$2,500** per covered individual.

### 4.2 Premium

Employee-only dental coverage is fully paid by the Company. Dependent dental coverage requires a monthly contribution of **$35** per dependent.

## 5. Vision Insurance

### 5.1 Coverage

5.1.1. Annual eye exam: $10 copay
5.1.2. Prescription lenses: covered in full (standard single, bifocal, or progressive)
5.1.3. Frames: up to **$200** allowance every 24 months
5.1.4. Contact lenses (in lieu of frames): up to **$150** allowance annually

### 5.2 Premium

Vision coverage is provided at no cost to the employee. Dependent vision coverage is **$12** per month.

## 6. Life and Disability Insurance

### 6.1 Basic Life Insurance

6.1.1. The Company provides basic life insurance at **2x annual base salary**, up to a maximum of $500,000.

6.1.2. This coverage is fully paid by the Company.

6.1.3. Accidental Death and Dismemberment (AD&D) coverage is included at an equal amount.

### 6.2 Supplemental Life Insurance

6.2.1. Employees may purchase supplemental life insurance in increments of $10,000 up to **5x annual base salary** (maximum $1,000,000).

6.2.2. Coverage amounts exceeding $250,000 require Evidence of Insurability (EOI).

6.2.3. Spousal and dependent child supplemental life insurance is also available.

### 6.3 Short-Term Disability (STD)

6.3.1. Benefit: **60% of weekly base salary**, up to $2,500 per week.

6.3.2. Elimination period: 7 calendar days.

6.3.3. Maximum benefit duration: **26 weeks**.

### 6.4 Long-Term Disability (LTD)

6.4.1. Benefit: **60% of monthly base salary**, up to $10,000 per month.

6.4.2. Elimination period: 180 calendar days (after STD exhaustion).

6.4.3. Maximum benefit duration: to Social Security Normal Retirement Age.

## 7. Retirement Benefits

### 7.1 401(k) Plan

7.1.1. All employees are eligible to participate in the Company's 401(k) plan upon hire.

7.1.2. Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limit on a pre-tax or Roth after-tax basis.

7.1.3. **Employer match:** The Company matches **100% of the first 3%** and **50% of the next 3%** of employee contributions, for a maximum employer match of **4.5%** of eligible compensation.

7.1.4. Employer contributions vest according to the following schedule:

| Years of Service | Vesting Percentage |
|------------------|--------------------|
| Less than 1 year | 0% |
| 1 year | 25% |
| 2 years | 50% |
| 3 years | 75% |
| 4+ years | 100% |

7.1.5. Auto-enrollment: New employees are automatically enrolled at a **6%** contribution rate unless they opt out or select a different rate.

### 7.2 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)

7.2.1. Eligible employees may purchase Company stock at a **15% discount** from the lower of the stock price at the beginning or end of each 6-month offering period.

7.2.2. Maximum annual contribution: **$25,000** or **15%** of eligible compensation, whichever is less.

7.2.3. Enrollment periods occur in **January** and **July** of each year.

7.2.4. Employees must have completed 90 days of employment to participate. See POL-004 Onboarding Guide, Section 7 for probation period details.

## 8. Wellness Benefits

### 8.1 Wellness Stipend

8.1.1. Each full-time employee receives a wellness stipend of **$1,200 per year** ($100 per month).

8.1.2. Eligible expenses include gym memberships, fitness classes, home exercise equipment, mental health apps, nutritional counseling, and ergonomic home office equipment.

8.1.3. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated stipend based on scheduled hours.

8.1.4. Reimbursement requests must be submitted through the expense management system with receipts within **60 days** of purchase.

### 8.2 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

8.2.1. All employees and their household members have access to the EAP at **no cost**.

8.2.2. The EAP provides up to **8 counseling sessions** per issue per year.

8.2.3. Services include mental health counseling, financial coaching, legal consultation, substance abuse support, and work-life resource referrals.

8.2.4. EAP services are **strictly confidential** and are not reported to the Company.

## 9. Education and Professional Development

### 9.1 Tuition Assistance

9.1.1. The Company provides up to **$5,250 per calendar year** in education assistance for approved courses, certifications, and degree programs related to the employee's current role or career path at the Company.

9.1.2. Employees must receive pre-approval from their manager and Human Resources before enrollment.

9.1.3. Reimbursement requires a grade of **B or higher** (or "Pass" in pass/fail courses).

9.1.4. Employees who voluntarily leave the Company within **12 months** of receiving education assistance must repay the full amount. Employees who leave within **12–24 months** must repay 50%.

### 9.2 Professional Certification

9.2.1. The Company covers exam fees and study materials for pre-approved professional certifications (e.g., PMP, AWS, CPA, SHRM).

9.2.2. This benefit is separate from the tuition assistance cap.

### 9.3 Conference Attendance

9.3.1. Employees may attend up to **2 professional conferences** per year with manager approval. Costs are covered by the department budget.

## 10. Commuter Benefits

10.1. The Company offers a pre-tax commuter benefit plan in accordance with IRS limits.

10.2. Monthly limits: **$300** for transit passes and **$300** for qualified parking.

10.3. Employees participating in the cycling commuter program may receive up to **$50 per month** for bicycle-related expenses.

10.4. For employees who work remotely, see POL-003 Remote Work Policy for internet reimbursement and equipment provisions.

## 11. Enrollment and Changes

### 11.1 Open Enrollment

11.1.1. Open enrollment occurs annually during **November 1–30** for coverage effective January 1 of the following year.

11.1.2. Employees who do not make elections during open enrollment will retain their current elections (with the exception of FSA contributions, which must be re-elected annually).

### 11.2 Qualifying Life Events

11.2.1. Employees may make mid-year benefit changes within **30 calendar days** of a qualifying life event, including:
- Marriage or divorce
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Death of a dependent
- Loss of other coverage
- Change in employment status

### 11.3 COBRA Continuation

11.3.1. Employees who lose coverage due to termination or reduction in hours may elect COBRA continuation coverage for up to **18 months** (or 36 months for certain qualifying events).

## 12. Benefit During Leave

12.1. During paid leave (vacation, sick, parental), all benefits continue without interruption.

12.2. During unpaid leave, employees may continue health, dental, and vision coverage by paying the full premium (employee + employer share). See POL-001 Vacation & Leave Policy, Section 10 for unpaid leave details.

12.3. Employer 401(k) matching contributions are suspended during unpaid leave.

## 13. Policy Administration

13.1. The Total Rewards team within Human Resources administers all benefit programs.

13.2. Detailed plan documents, Summary Plan Descriptions (SPDs), and enrollment forms are available on the Company intranet.

13.3. Questions should be directed to benefits@meridiantech.com or ext. 4500.

13.4. Benefit levels described in this handbook are subject to annual review. Employees will receive notice of changes during open enrollment. This handbook is a summary; in the event of a conflict, the official plan documents govern.
