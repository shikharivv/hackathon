# Retail Shift Scheduling and Breaks

**Policy Number:** POL-009
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

Fictional policy for the Meridian retail hackathon demo; not real company guidance.

## 1. Viewing the Roster

View your scheduled shifts in the employee HRIS under My Roster. Check your store, date, start time, finish time, and supervisor. Ask your shift supervisor about discrepancies. This chatbot explains the policy but does not have access to your personal roster.

## 2. Requesting a Shift Swap

Find a trained colleague who can cover the role and is willing to swap. Submit a Shift Swap request in the employee HRIS with both employees' names, dates, and proposed shifts. Your shift supervisor must approve the change before either employee works the new shift. An informal agreement alone does not change the roster.

## 3. Availability Changes

Submit an Availability Change request in the employee HRIS with the dates and hours you can work. Discuss the request with your store manager. Changes depend on role coverage and the confirmed roster; submitting a request does not guarantee approval.

## 4. Overtime Approval

Ask your shift supervisor or store manager to approve planned overtime before working it. Record all time actually worked, including unexpected extra time, accurately in the timekeeping system. Payroll applies the company's applicable pay rules. The assistant cannot calculate your overtime pay or determine legal entitlements.

## 5. Break Arrangements

Your shift supervisor schedules rest and meal breaks according to the applicable roster and local requirements. Check the roster or ask the supervisor for your break times; this demo policy does not set a universal break duration. If coverage prevents a scheduled break, tell the supervisor immediately so another arrangement can be made.

## 6. Holiday and Peak Trading Shifts

Store opening hours and peak trading coverage can affect rosters. Check your assigned holiday shifts in the employee HRIS and ask your store manager about coverage. Holiday pay is documented in the Vacation and Leave Policy. Requests for time off still use the normal leave approval process.
