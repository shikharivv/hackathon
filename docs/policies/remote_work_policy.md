# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Version:** 2.1
**Department:** Human Resources — Workplace Operations
**Approved By:** Chief People Officer & Chief Operating Officer
**Last Reviewed:** October 20, 2023
**Next Review:** October 20, 2024

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for remote and hybrid work arrangements at Meridian Technologies, Inc. ("the Company"). It defines eligibility, expectations, equipment provisions, and compliance requirements to ensure productivity, collaboration, and employee well-being in a flexible work environment.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees. Specific roles may be designated as on-site only based on business requirements. Contractors and temporary workers should refer to their engagement agreements for remote work provisions.

## 3. Work Models

### 3.1 Hybrid Model (Default)

3.1.1. The Company's default work arrangement is a hybrid model consisting of **3 days in the office** and **2 days working remotely** per week.

3.1.2. In-office days are determined at the team level by the department manager and must include at least one common team day for collaboration.

3.1.3. Employees may request adjustments to their in-office schedule through their manager, subject to business needs.

### 3.2 Fully Remote

3.2.1. Fully remote arrangements are available for eligible roles where physical office presence is not required for job performance.

3.2.2. Eligibility criteria for fully remote work:
- The role has been designated as "remote-eligible" by the department head
- The employee has completed the 90-day probation period (see POL-004 Onboarding Guide, Section 7)
- The employee has received a performance rating of 3 ("Meets Expectations") or higher in the most recent review cycle (see POL-005 Performance Review Policy, Section 4)
- The employee's home office meets the safety requirements outlined in Section 8

3.2.3. Fully remote employees must be able to travel to the nearest Company office for mandatory in-person events with **2 weeks' notice**.

### 3.3 Fully On-Site

3.3.1. Certain roles (e.g., facilities management, lab technicians, reception) require full-time on-site presence.

3.3.2. On-site employees are not eligible for the internet reimbursement (Section 6) but receive all other benefits.

## 4. Equipment Provision

### 4.1 Company-Provided Equipment

4.1.1. All remote and hybrid employees receive the following Company-issued equipment:
- Laptop computer (refreshed every 3 years)
- External monitor (24" minimum)
- External keyboard and mouse
- Headset with microphone

4.1.2. Company equipment remains the property of Meridian Technologies and must be returned upon termination of employment or transition to an on-site role.

4.1.3. Equipment repairs and replacements for normal wear are covered by the Company. Damage due to negligence may be charged to the employee.

### 4.2 Home Office Setup Allowance

4.2.1. New remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time **$500 home office setup allowance** to purchase ergonomic furniture, lighting, or other workspace improvements.

4.2.2. This allowance is provided through expense reimbursement and must be claimed within **90 days** of the employee's start date or approval of a remote work arrangement.

4.2.3. Eligible purchases include desks, chairs, monitor stands, lighting, cable management, and whiteboards.

4.2.4. Employees who transition from hybrid to fully remote receive an additional **$300 supplemental allowance**.

## 5. Software and Access

5.1. Remote employees must use Company-approved collaboration tools, including the corporate VPN, for all work-related activities.

5.2. Personal devices may not be used for accessing Company systems unless enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) program.

5.3. Employees must adhere to the Company's Information Security Policy at all times. See POL-006 Code of Conduct, Section 5 for confidentiality and data protection requirements.

## 6. Internet Reimbursement

6.1. Remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a monthly internet reimbursement of **$75**.

6.2. To qualify, the employee must maintain a broadband connection with a minimum speed of **50 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload**.

6.3. Reimbursement is processed through the monthly payroll cycle and is taxable income unless otherwise determined by applicable tax law.

6.4. Employees must submit proof of internet service (bill or statement) upon initial enrollment and annually thereafter.

## 7. Core Hours and Availability

### 7.1 Core Hours

7.1.1. All employees, regardless of work location, must be available during **core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM** in their local time zone.

7.1.2. Outside of core hours, employees may arrange their schedules flexibly, provided they meet the expectations of their role and complete their standard work hours (typically 40 hours per week for full-time employees).

### 7.2 Response Time Expectations

7.2.1. During core hours, employees are expected to respond to messages and emails within **1 hour**.

7.2.2. Outside core hours, responses within **4 hours** during business days are expected for non-urgent matters.

7.2.3. Employees must keep their calendar up-to-date and set status indicators in collaboration tools to reflect their availability.

### 7.3 Meeting Expectations

7.3.1. Cameras should be **on by default** for team meetings and client calls unless bandwidth or other constraints require otherwise.

7.3.2. Employees should join meetings from a quiet, professional environment.

7.3.3. Recurring team meetings should include at least one option within core hours to accommodate all time zones.

## 8. Home Office Safety Requirements

8.1. Remote employees must maintain a dedicated workspace that meets basic safety standards:
- Adequate lighting (minimum 300 lux at the work surface)
- Ergonomic setup (adjustable chair, monitor at eye level, keyboard at elbow height)
- Smoke detector and fire extinguisher accessible within the work area
- Unobstructed emergency exit path
- Electrical outlets in good condition (no frayed cords or overloaded circuits)

8.2. Employees must complete the **Home Office Safety Self-Assessment** checklist during onboarding and annually thereafter. This form is available on the Company intranet.

8.3. The Company reserves the right to request photographs or conduct a virtual inspection of the home office environment.

8.4. Work-related injuries sustained in the home office during work hours are covered under workers' compensation. Employees must report any injuries immediately through the standard incident reporting process.

## 9. International Remote Work

### 9.1 Short-Term International Work

9.1.1. Employees may request to work remotely from a location outside their country of employment for a maximum of **30 calendar days per year**.

9.1.2. Requests must be submitted at least **30 days in advance** and require approval from the employee's manager, Human Resources, and the Legal/Tax team.

### 9.2 Tax and Legal Implications

9.2.1. International remote work may trigger tax obligations in the host country for both the employee and the Company. The employee is responsible for understanding and complying with local tax requirements.

9.2.2. The Company will not establish a tax presence (permanent establishment) in a foreign jurisdiction due to an employee's remote work arrangement.

9.2.3. Employees may not work from countries subject to comprehensive U.S. sanctions.

### 9.3 Data Compliance

9.3.1. Employees working internationally must ensure compliance with local data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR in the EU).

9.3.2. Access to certain Company systems may be restricted based on the employee's location due to data sovereignty requirements.

## 10. Performance Monitoring

10.1. Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as on-site employees. Performance is measured by output and results, not by hours logged.

10.2. Managers are expected to conduct regular **1:1 meetings** (minimum bi-weekly) with remote team members.

10.3. The Company may use productivity analytics tools on Company-issued devices. Any monitoring tools will be disclosed to employees in advance.

10.4. Performance issues will be addressed through the standard performance management process outlined in POL-005 Performance Review Policy.

## 11. Expenses and Reimbursement

11.1. In addition to the internet reimbursement (Section 6) and setup allowance (Section 4.2), the following remote work expenses are reimbursable:
- Office supplies (pens, paper, printer ink): up to **$25/month**
- Software or tools required for work (with manager pre-approval)
- Travel to the office for mandatory in-person events (for fully remote employees)

11.2. All reimbursement requests must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense through the Company's expense management system.

11.3. The wellness stipend described in POL-002 Benefits Handbook, Section 8.1 may also be applied toward ergonomic equipment.

## 12. Return to Office

### 12.1 Voluntary Return

12.1.1. Employees with remote arrangements may voluntarily transition to a hybrid or on-site arrangement by notifying their manager and Human Resources with **2 weeks' notice**.

### 12.2 Company-Initiated Return

12.2.1. The Company reserves the right to require employees to return to the office or adjust their work arrangement based on business needs.

12.2.2. Employees will be provided **30 calendar days' notice** for any Company-initiated change to their work arrangement.

12.2.3. Relocation assistance may be provided at the Company's discretion for fully remote employees required to relocate closer to an office.

### 12.3 Revocation of Remote Privileges

12.3.1. Remote work privileges may be revoked for the following reasons:
- Performance rating below 3 ("Below Expectations")
- Violation of this policy or the Information Security Policy
- Change in role requirements
- Failure to maintain required internet speeds or home office safety standards

12.3.2. Revocation of remote privileges is not considered a disciplinary action but may coincide with performance management actions under POL-005 Performance Review Policy.

## 13. Communication and Culture

13.1. Teams with remote members should establish a **team charter** documenting communication norms, meeting cadence, and collaboration expectations.

13.2. The Company organizes **quarterly in-person gatherings** (team offsites, all-hands meetings) to maintain culture and connection. Attendance is expected for all employees, including fully remote workers.

13.3. Remote employees are encouraged to participate in virtual social events, mentoring programs, and employee resource groups.

## 14. Policy Administration

14.1. Human Resources and the Workplace Operations team jointly administer this policy.

14.2. Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the department head and CHRO.

14.3. Questions should be directed to workplaceops@meridiantech.com.
