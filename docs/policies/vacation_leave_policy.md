## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the guidelines and procedures for all forms of paid and unpaid leave available to eligible employees of Meridian Technologies, Inc. ("the Company"). It ensures fair and consistent administration of time-off benefits while maintaining operational efficiency.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees. Temporary employees, contractors, and consultants are not eligible for leave benefits under this policy unless otherwise specified by their engagement agreement.

## 3. Annual Leave Entitlement

### 3.1 Full-Time Employees

Annual leave is accrued on a per-pay-period basis and varies by length of continuous service:

| Tenure | Annual Leave Days | Accrual Rate (per pay period) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| 0 – 2 years | 15 days (120 hours) | 4.62 hours |
| 3 – 7 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 6.15 hours |
| 8+ years | 25 days (200 hours) | 7.69 hours |

### 3.2 Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis calculated as follows:

> **Pro-rata entitlement** = (Weekly contracted hours / 40) x Full-time entitlement

For example, an employee working 24 hours per week with 2 years of tenure would receive:
(24 / 40) x 15 = 9 days of annual leave.

### 3.3 Accrual Start Date

Leave accrual begins on the employee's first day of employment. However, employees may not use accrued leave during the first 90 days of employment (probation period). See POL-004 Onboarding Guide, Section 7 for probation period details.

## 4. Leave Request Procedure

### 4.1 Advance Notice

4.1.1. Employees must submit leave requests a minimum of **14 calendar days** in advance for planned absences of 3 or more consecutive days.

4.1.2. For absences of 1–2 days, a minimum of **5 business days** advance notice is required.

4.1.3. Emergency or unforeseen absences must be reported to the employee's direct manager no later than **1 hour before** the scheduled start of the workday.

### 4.2 Approval Process

4.2.1. All leave requests must be submitted through the Company's HRIS (Human Resource Information System).

4.2.2. The employee's direct manager is responsible for approving or denying leave requests within **3 business days** of submission.

4.2.3. Requests for leave exceeding 10 consecutive business days require additional approval from the department head.

4.2.4. Managers must consider team coverage, project deadlines, and operational needs when evaluating requests.

### 4.3 Denial of Leave

4.3.1. Managers may deny leave requests due to business necessity, insufficient staffing coverage, or conflicts with blackout periods (see Section 9).

4.3.2. Employees may appeal denied requests through Human Resources within 5 business days of the denial.

## 5. Carry-Over Rules

### 5.1 Annual Carry-Over Limit

5.1.1. Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused leave days** (40 hours) into the following calendar year.

5.1.2. Carried-over days must be used by **March 31** of the new calendar year. Any unused carried-over days will be forfeited after this date.

5.1.3. The Company does not provide monetary compensation for forfeited leave days except where required by applicable state law.

### 5.2 Exceptions

5.2.1. Employees who were unable to use leave due to documented business necessity may request an extension of the carry-over deadline by submitting a written request to Human Resources before February 15.

5.2.2. Employees on approved medical or parental leave at year-end will have their carry-over deadline extended by the duration of their leave.

## 6. Paid Holidays

### 6.1 Statutory Holidays

The Company observes the following **10 paid holidays** per calendar year:

1. New Year's Day — January 1
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day — Third Monday in January
3. Presidents' Day — Third Monday in February
4. Memorial Day — Last Monday in May
5. Independence Day — July 4
6. Labor Day — First Monday in September
7. Thanksgiving Day — Fourth Thursday in November
8. Day After Thanksgiving — Fourth Friday in November
9. Christmas Eve — December 24
10. Christmas Day — December 25

### 6.2 Floating Holiday

6.2.1. Each employee receives **1 floating holiday** per calendar year to be used at the employee's discretion.

6.2.2. Floating holidays do not carry over and must be used within the calendar year.

### 6.3 Holiday Pay for Non-Exempt Employees

Non-exempt employees required to work on a paid holiday will receive their regular rate of pay plus a holiday premium of **1.5x** their hourly rate for all hours worked.

## 7. Special Leave

### 7.1 Bereavement Leave

7.1.1. Employees are entitled to **5 paid business days** of bereavement leave upon the death of an immediate family member (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or grandchild).

7.1.2. Employees are entitled to **3 paid business days** for the death of an extended family member (aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, cousin, in-law).

7.1.3. Additional unpaid leave may be approved at the manager's discretion.

### 7.2 Jury Duty

7.2.1. Employees summoned for jury duty will receive their regular pay for up to **10 business days** per calendar year.

7.2.2. Employees must provide a copy of the jury summons to their manager and Human Resources as soon as it is received.

7.2.3. Employees are expected to return to work on any day they are dismissed early from jury service.

### 7.3 Marriage Leave

7.3.1. Employees are entitled to **3 paid business days** of marriage leave for their own wedding.

7.3.2. This leave must be taken within 30 calendar days of the marriage date.

7.3.3. Marriage leave is a one-time benefit per marriage event.

### 7.4 Voting Leave

7.4.1. Employees are granted up to **2 paid hours** on Election Day to exercise their right to vote, where not otherwise provided by existing work schedule flexibility.

## 8. Parental Leave

### 8.1 Primary Caregiver Leave

8.1.1. Employees designated as the primary caregiver are entitled to **16 weeks** of paid parental leave at 100% of base salary.

8.1.2. This leave applies to birth, adoption, or foster care placement.

8.1.3. Primary caregiver leave runs concurrently with any applicable FMLA entitlement.

### 8.2 Secondary Caregiver Leave

8.2.1. Employees designated as the secondary caregiver are entitled to **4 weeks** of paid parental leave at 100% of base salary.

8.2.2. Secondary caregiver leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.

### 8.3 Eligibility

8.3.1. Employees must have completed a minimum of **12 months** of continuous employment to be eligible for parental leave benefits.

8.3.2. Employees with less than 12 months of service may be eligible for unpaid FMLA leave where applicable.

### 8.4 Return to Work

8.4.1. Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed the same or a comparable position with equivalent pay and benefits.

8.4.2. Employees must confirm their return date with their manager and Human Resources at least **2 weeks** before the end of their leave.

## 9. Blackout Periods

### 9.1 Fiscal Year-End Blackout

9.1.1. A leave blackout period is in effect during the Company's fiscal year-end close, typically the **last 5 business days of the fiscal year** and the **first 5 business days of the new fiscal year**.

9.1.2. During blackout periods, vacation requests will only be approved under exceptional circumstances with written approval from the department head and CFO.

### 9.2 Department-Specific Blackouts

9.2.1. Individual departments may designate additional blackout periods based on seasonal business demands.

9.2.2. Department-specific blackout dates must be communicated to all affected employees at least **30 calendar days** in advance and must be approved by Human Resources.

## 10. Unpaid Leave of Absence

### 10.1 Eligibility

10.1.1. Employees who have exhausted all paid leave options may request an unpaid leave of absence for personal reasons.

10.1.2. Unpaid leave requests must be submitted in writing to Human Resources at least **30 calendar days** in advance.

### 10.2 Duration

10.2.1. Unpaid leave may be granted for a maximum of **12 weeks** within a rolling 12-month period.

10.2.2. During unpaid leave, benefit continuation is subject to the terms outlined in POL-002 Benefits Handbook, Section 10.

### 10.3 Impact on Employment

10.3.1. Unpaid leave of absence does not count toward tenure calculations for purposes of annual leave accrual (Section 3.1).

10.3.2. Employees on unpaid leave remain subject to all Company policies, including POL-006 Code of Conduct.

## 11. Leave During Notice Period

11.1. Employees who have submitted their resignation may not use accrued vacation during the notice period without prior written approval from their manager and Human Resources.

11.2. Upon termination of employment, accrued but unused vacation will be paid out in the final paycheck in accordance with applicable state law.

## 12. Abuse of Leave Policy

12.1. Patterns of leave abuse, including but not limited to frequent unplanned absences, absences surrounding holidays or weekends, or falsification of leave reasons, will be addressed through the disciplinary process outlined in POL-006 Code of Conduct, Section 10.

12.2. The Company reserves the right to require medical documentation for absences of 3 or more consecutive days or for suspected abuse.

## 13. Recordkeeping

13.1. All leave records are maintained in the HRIS and retained for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with applicable regulations.

13.2. Employees may view their leave balances and history at any time through the employee self-service portal.

## 14. Policy Administration

14.1. Human Resources is responsible for the administration and interpretation of this policy.

14.2. Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the HR Benefits team at benefits@meridiantech.com.
