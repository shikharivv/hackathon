## 1. Purpose

This guide outlines the structured onboarding process for all new hires at Meridian Technologies, Inc. ("the Company"). A consistent onboarding experience ensures that new employees are set up for success, understand Company culture and policies, and become productive members of their teams as quickly as possible.

## 2. Scope

This guide applies to all new regular full-time and part-time employees. Modified onboarding processes may apply to contractors, interns, and temporary workers as defined by their engagement agreements.

## 3. Pre-Boarding (Before Day 1)

### 3.1 HR Pre-Boarding Tasks

Upon acceptance of the offer letter, Human Resources will initiate the following:

3.1.1. **Background Check and Verification** — Conditional offer contingent on satisfactory results; typically completed within 5–7 business days.

3.1.2. **New Hire Paperwork** — The following documents will be sent electronically for completion prior to Day 1:
- I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification
- W-4 Federal Tax Withholding
- State tax withholding form (where applicable)
- Direct deposit authorization
- Emergency contact information
- Confidentiality and non-disclosure agreement
- Acknowledgment of receipt of Employee Handbook

3.1.3. **Benefits Pre-Enrollment** — New hires will receive benefits information and may begin reviewing plan options prior to Day 1. See POL-002 Benefits Handbook for full details on available plans and coverage tiers.

### 3.2 IT Setup

3.2.1. The IT department will provision the following based on the hiring manager's request:
- Laptop computer (shipped to employee's address for remote workers)
- Email account and corporate directory listing
- Access credentials for core systems (HRIS, project management, collaboration tools)
- VPN access for remote and hybrid employees
- Phone extension (for on-site roles)

3.2.2. For remote and hybrid employees, equipment will be shipped to arrive at least **2 business days** before the start date. See POL-003 Remote Work Policy, Section 4 for full equipment details.

### 3.3 Facilities Setup

3.3.1. **Building access badge** — Issued on Day 1 at the security desk (on-site employees).

3.3.2. **Parking assignment** — Allocated based on availability; new hires may be placed on a waitlist for premium spots.

3.3.3. **Workspace assignment** — Desk or office assignment confirmed by the hiring manager at least 3 business days before start date.

3.3.4. **Welcome kit** — Includes Company-branded items, office supplies, and a printed quick-start reference card.

## 5. Week 1 Milestones

By the end of the first week, new employees should have completed the following:

### 5.1 System Access Verification

- [ ] Corporate email configured and functional
- [ ] HRIS profile completed (personal information, emergency contacts, tax forms)
- [ ] VPN connectivity confirmed (remote/hybrid employees)
- [ ] Project management tool access granted
- [ ] Department-specific systems access granted
- [ ] Intranet login verified

### 5.2 Required Training Modules

The following training modules must be completed within the **first 7 calendar days**:

| Module | Duration | Delivery |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Information Security Awareness | 45 min | Online (self-paced) |
| Anti-Harassment Prevention | 60 min | Online (self-paced) |
| Data Privacy and GDPR Basics | 30 min | Online (self-paced) |
| Workplace Safety | 30 min | Online (self-paced) |
| Code of Conduct Overview | 20 min | Online (self-paced) |

5.2.1. All training modules are accessed through the Learning Management System (LMS).

5.2.2. Completion records are tracked automatically and reported to Human Resources.

5.2.3. Employees who do not complete required training within the deadline will receive a reminder from HR; failure to complete within **14 calendar days** will be escalated to the employee's manager.

### 5.3 Key Meetings

- [ ] 1:1 with direct manager (initial expectations discussion)
- [ ] Introduction meeting with skip-level manager
- [ ] Meet-and-greet with key cross-functional stakeholders
- [ ] Buddy/mentor introductory coffee chat

## 6. 30/60/90 Day Plan

### 6.1 First 30 Days — Learn

**Objective:** Build foundational knowledge of the role, team, and Company.

6.1.1. Complete all required compliance training modules.

6.1.2. Review relevant Company policies, including:
- POL-001 Vacation & Leave Policy
- POL-002 Benefits Handbook
- POL-003 Remote Work Policy
- POL-005 Performance Review Policy
- POL-006 Code of Conduct

6.1.3. Shadow team members on key workflows and projects.

6.1.4. Attend all scheduled team meetings and recurring syncs.

6.1.5. Complete initial goal-setting discussion with manager.

6.1.6. **30-Day Check-In:** Formal meeting with manager to review progress, address questions, and adjust onboarding plan as needed.

### 6.2 First 60 Days — Contribute

**Objective:** Begin contributing to team deliverables and building relationships.

6.2.1. Take ownership of at least one assigned task or workstream.

6.2.2. Participate actively in team projects and meetings.

6.2.3. Provide feedback on the onboarding experience to HR through the 60-day survey.

6.2.4. Begin working toward goals established in the 30-day check-in.

6.2.5. **60-Day Check-In:** Formal meeting with manager to assess integration, performance trajectory, and any support needs.

### 6.3 First 90 Days — Own

**Objective:** Demonstrate independence in role responsibilities and meet initial performance milestones.

6.3.1. Manage assigned workstreams with minimal oversight.

6.3.2. Demonstrate understanding of team processes, tools, and standards.

6.3.3. Build productive working relationships with key stakeholders.

6.3.4. Complete the probation period review (see Section 7).

6.3.5. **90-Day Check-In:** Formal performance review marking the end of the probation period.

## 7. Probation Period

### 7.1 Duration

7.1.1. All new employees are subject to a **90-calendar-day probation period** beginning on their start date.

7.1.2. The probation period may be extended by up to **30 additional days** if the manager determines that more time is needed to evaluate the employee's fit and performance. Extensions must be documented and approved by Human Resources.

### 7.2 Expectations During Probation

7.2.1. Employees are expected to demonstrate reliability, willingness to learn, alignment with Company values, and progress toward role-specific competencies.

7.2.2. During the probation period, employees may not use accrued vacation leave (see POL-001 Vacation & Leave Policy, Section 3.3). Sick leave and emergency absences are permitted.

7.2.3. Employees on probation are not eligible for fully remote work arrangements (see POL-003 Remote Work Policy, Section 3.2.2).

### 7.3 Probation Review

7.3.1. At the end of the probation period, the manager will conduct a formal review covering:
- Attendance and reliability
- Quality of work output
- Adherence to Company policies
- Cultural fit and teamwork
- Progress on 30/60/90-day plan milestones

7.3.2. Possible outcomes:
- **Confirmation:** Employee is confirmed as a regular employee with full access to all benefits and programs.
- **Extension:** Probation extended with a documented improvement plan.
- **Termination:** Employment is terminated during or at the end of the probation period. The Company may terminate probationary employees with **1 week's notice** or pay in lieu thereof.

## 8. Buddy and Mentor Program

### 8.1 Buddy Assignment

8.1.1. Each new hire is assigned a **buddy** from their team or a closely related team within the first week.

8.1.2. The buddy serves as an informal resource for questions about day-to-day operations, team norms, and Company culture.

8.1.3. Buddies are expected to check in with their new hire at least **twice per week** during the first month and **weekly** during months 2 and 3.

8.1.4. Buddy assignments last for **90 days** and coincide with the probation period.

### 8.2 Mentor Assignment

8.2.1. Employees may be assigned a **mentor** from outside their direct team for longer-term career guidance.

8.2.2. Mentor assignments are typically made within the first **60 days** and last for **12 months**.

8.2.3. Mentors and mentees are expected to meet at least **monthly** and establish goals for the mentoring relationship.

## 9. Required Compliance Training

### 9.1 Initial Training (Within First 7 Days)

Refer to Section 5.2 for the list of required modules to be completed during the first week.

### 9.2 Extended Training (Within First 30 Days)

The following additional training must be completed within **30 calendar days** of the start date:

| Module | Duration | Delivery |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Insider Trading Awareness | 30 min | Online (self-paced) |
| Anti-Bribery and Corruption | 30 min | Online (self-paced) |
| Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion | 45 min | Online (self-paced) |
| Emergency Procedures and Evacuation | 20 min | On-site or online |
| Manager Essentials (people managers only) | 90 min | Instructor-led |

### 9.3 Annual Refresher Training

9.3.1. Certain compliance training modules require annual renewal. Employees will receive automated reminders through the LMS 30 days before the renewal deadline.

9.3.2. Failure to complete annual refresher training may affect the employee's performance review. See POL-005 Performance Review Policy for details.
