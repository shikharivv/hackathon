# Retail Employee Services and Training

**Policy Number:** POL-010
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

Fictional policy for the Meridian retail hackathon demo; not real company guidance.

## 1. Uniform Requests

Ask your store manager for the required uniform and identification badge for your role. Submit a Uniform Request in the employee HRIS for an initial issue, replacement, or size change. State the item and size; report a damaged safety item immediately. This demo does not specify an employee charge or reimbursement amount.

## 2. Employee Discount

Use your own employee account or approved staff identification at the checkout. The applicable discount and exclusions are shown in the employee benefits portal. Do not lend your staff account or badge to someone else. Ask your store manager about a rejected discount; this policy does not provide a universal discount percentage.

## 3. Required Training

Complete the induction, store safety, data protection, and customer service modules assigned in the employee learning portal. Your store manager explains role-specific modules and schedules time for required training. Contact the HR Support Desk if a module is missing or inaccessible.

## 4. Updating Personal Details

In the employee HRIS, open My Profile to update your address, phone number, or emergency contact. Use the secure portal for personal records. If a field is locked, submit a Profile Update request to HR Support; do not put private identification numbers into the chatbot.

## 5. Lost Badge or System Access

Report a lost badge to your store manager immediately so the access can be reviewed. Use the approved IT support channel for a locked account or password reset. Never share passwords or verification codes. HR Support can help identify the correct support team.

## 6. Leaving the Company

Send your resignation through the employee HRIS or the documented HR channel and discuss handover with your manager. The notice period is the one in your employment agreement; this demo policy does not set a single notice period. HR coordinates final records and return of company property.
